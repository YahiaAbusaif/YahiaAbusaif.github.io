I am Yahia Ali Abusaif, a computer engineer and a former teacher. I want to make a fresh start here on what this blog is about.

**H**ere is more information about me: I am an Arabian Muslim man who lived in Saudi Arabia and now lives in Egypt, Arabic is my first language and English is my second language, I have experience in programming, problem-solving, losing weight, and teaching kids, I love to share these beside some books that I love.

**I** love writing my thoughts in my notes, but most of the time it was like random points here and there and I hadn't high motivation before to organize and write them in a readable way, that was my point to begin writing articles, I mainly want to rewrite some points from my experience or things I have read, trying to write it in a way that is easy to read and understand so I can share it, get feedback, edit it and so on until I find I have a clear understanding on this topic.

**T**here are many reasons why I am starting this blog but let's say I tried before to use social media websites however the majority of the users on social media don't enter to read long articles, they prefer short paragraphs with fewer Ideas as they are opening these platforms in relaxing and free time.

**I** know looking at the current state and trend in the world today is creating videos and there are a minority of people that prefer reading over watching however I still believe this is still the better option for me in 2022.

**A**nother Reason is to motivate me to read and learn more about the topic I want to write about. I will try to add this project to my calendar so there are frequent new posts each month.

**I** had some topics in my mind that covered several things like programming, soft skills, book summary, lifestyle, philosophy, religious thought, ideologies discussion, and social life, mainly focusing on college students and maybe senior secondary students.

**I** am sure things are goanna improve with time, I am still discovering the blog's options and this is just the beginning for me.
